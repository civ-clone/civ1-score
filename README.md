# civ1-score

Civilization's Civilization Score, as `core-score` rules, as Civ1 v474.05 adds it up:

- **citizens**: 2 for each happy citizen, 1 for each content one or specialist, counted at 40% luxuries;
- **wonders**: 20 for each Wonder, obsolete or not;
- **spaceship**: once it has landed, its population (in tens of thousands) × its chance of success (%) ÷ 2;
- **pollution**: −10 for each polluted square in the world;
- **peace**: 3 for each turn of world peace in the years AD, at most 100;
- **future-technology**: 5 for each Future Technology.

It also keeps the world's peace: a turn of it at the start of each turn in the years AD, ended by any fight.
